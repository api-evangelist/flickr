Profile of Virginia-based documentary photographer Aeysa Nixon-Bright, whose family's early camera use taught her a deep lesson in the power of the image. The interview covers her focus on community and underrepresented stories, a personal grief project from 2015, and her involvement with Black Women Photographers.
