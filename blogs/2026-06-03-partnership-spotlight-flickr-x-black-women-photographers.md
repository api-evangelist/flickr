Flickr celebrates its evolving partnership with Black Women Photographers, which has grown since 2022 to include increasing grant amounts from $1,200 to $5,000, professional memberships, and expanded visibility opportunities. The article highlights three grant recipients and their award-winning photography projects exploring themes of identity, transformation, and belonging.
